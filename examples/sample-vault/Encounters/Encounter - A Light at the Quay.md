# Encounter - A Light at the Quay

**Scene**: Lantern Quay
**NPCs**: [[Mira Reed]]
**Type**: Social
**Difficulty**: Low
**Duration**: 15 minutes
**Location**: [[Lantern Quay]]

## Overview

The keeper offers a brass token for news of the missing lens.

## Leads

- A trail of blue wax leads toward the old ferry.
- [[Tavi Finch]] heard a bell after midnight.
- The [[Lantern Keepers]] have a spare lens, but it is cracked.

## Resolution

Return the lens, negotiate a replacement, or signal the boats from shore.
The ferry pilot borrowed it to guide a stranded fishing boat and plans to
return it at dawn. There need not be a villain or a fight.
